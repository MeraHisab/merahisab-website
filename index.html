About MeraHisab

MeraHisab is a personal finance management app created to make everyday money tracking simple and convenient.

Whether you want to record income, manage expenses, organize accounts, review reports or keep track of your savings, MeraHisab brings these activities together in one place.

Our goal is to provide a clean, useful and privacy-focused financial management experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about MeraHisab.

1. What is MeraHisab?

MeraHisab is a personal finance app that helps you record income, expenses, savings and manage your accounts.

2. Does MeraHisab work offline?

The app is designed to support local, offline financial data management.

3. Where is my financial data stored?

Financial records are designed to be stored locally on your device.

4. Can I create reports?

Yes. You can generate financial reports and export them in supported formats.

5. Can I back up my data?

Yes. The app includes backup and restore functionality for your records.

6. Is MeraHisab available on Android?

MeraHisab is being developed for Android. Availability and download details will be updated here.
