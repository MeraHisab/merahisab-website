HELP & INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions.

Find answers to common questions about MeraHisab and Royal Khata.

What is MeraHisab?
MeraHisab is a personal finance application that helps organize income, expenses, savings and account balances.
What is Royal Khata?
Royal Khata is designed for digital khata management and organized credit and debit records. Visit the Royal Khata page for more information.
Can I track income and expenses?
MeraHisab is designed to record income and expenses and review financial activity through available reports.
Does MeraHisab support multiple accounts?
MeraHisab is designed to organize finances into separate accounts and subcategories.
Can I create a backup?
MeraHisab includes backup and restore functionality for managing saved records. Keep important backup files in a safe location.
Where is my personal data stored?
MeraHisab is designed for local data storage. Read our Privacy Policy for further information about data handling and backup files.
Can I install the website on my phone?
The website includes Progressive Web App support. Installation availability depends on your browser, device and successful PWA configuration. Website installation is separate from installing the Android APK.
Can I use the website without internet?
Previously cached public pages may remain accessible offline after successful PWA setup. Downloads, uncached resources and some other features may require an internet connection.
Where can I download the applications?
Visit the official Download Center for available application downloads and release information.
Is Royal Khata available for download?
Royal Khata download information is currently pending. An official download link will be provided when available.
Are financial calculators available?
Visit the Financial Tools page to explore available calculators and time-management tools. Calculator results are estimates for informational purposes.
How can I contact MeraHisab support?
Visit our Contact & Support page for available support options. The website does not currently guarantee automatic delivery of support requests.
