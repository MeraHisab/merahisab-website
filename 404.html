Frequently Asked Questions

What does a 404 error mean?

It means that the requested website resource could not be found.

Is the entire website unavailable?

Not necessarily. A single page may be unavailable while other website pages continue to work. Try opening the homepage.

Will this error delete my financial records?

Displaying this website error does not itself delete native Android application records. Do not clear application storage without securing a backup.

Can I still download MeraHisab?

Visit the official Download Center for available APK download and installation information.

Where can I find Royal Khata?

Visit the Royal Khata application information page. Its official APK release is pending.

Why is an offline page unavailable?

Offline access depends on successful service worker installation and previously cached resources. Not every website page is available offline.

Should I clear my browser cache?

First try opening the homepage or refreshing the requested page. Clearing website storage may remove preferences and offline resources.

How can I report an incorrect link?

Copy the local error report and visit the Contact & Support page to prepare a support request. Direct support submission is pending configuration.
