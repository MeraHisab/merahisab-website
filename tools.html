Frequently Asked Questions

Are these calculators free?

The calculators on this website can be used without a calculator subscription.

What is EMI?

EMI means Equated Monthly Instalment. The EMI calculator estimates fixed monthly loan repayments using the reducing-balance interest formula.

What is SIP?

SIP means Systematic Investment Plan. The calculator estimates the future value of regular monthly investments using an assumed annual return. Actual market returns are not guaranteed.

What is the difference between FD and RD?

A fixed deposit generally begins with a lump-sum deposit. A recurring deposit uses regular contributions over time. Bank-specific calculation rules may differ.

Can I calculate GST?

Yes. The GST calculator supports adding GST to a base amount and extracting GST from a tax-inclusive amount. Enter the applicable tax rate yourself.

Are investment returns guaranteed?

No. Investment calculators use assumed rates for illustrative estimates. They do not predict actual market returns.

Does the calculator save my financial details?

The calculator engine does not persist your entered amounts or results. Your browser may independently retain information according to its settings.

Can I use the tools without internet?

Offline use depends on successful PWA installation or caching of the required website resources.

Can I save calculator results as PDF?

Use the Print / Save as PDF button. PDF availability depends on your browser and operating system.

Is the IST clock synchronized with an official time server?

No. It displays Indian Standard Time using your device clock and browser time-zone conversion.
