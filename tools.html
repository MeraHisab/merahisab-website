Frequently Asked Questions

Are these calculators free?

Yes. All 11 financial calculators on this website are available without payment.

What is EMI?

EMI means Equated Monthly Instalment. It includes loan principal and interest. This calculator estimates EMI using a fixed interest rate and reducing balance.

What is SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan involves investing a fixed amount regularly. The calculator estimates future value using an assumed return, which is not guaranteed.

What is the difference between FD and RD?

An FD involves a lump-sum deposit, while an RD involves regular monthly deposits. Actual bank interest and maturity rules may differ.

Can I calculate GST?

Yes. Enter your amount and applicable GST rate to add GST or extract included GST. The calculator does not determine which tax rate applies.

Are investment returns guaranteed?

No. SIP, FD, RD and other investment calculations are estimates based on entered values and mathematical assumptions.

Does the calculator save my financial details?

The calculator code does not persistently store or upload financial inputs. Browser form and printing behavior may apply separately.

Can I use the tools without internet?

Calculators can work offline if the required website resources have already been cached successfully.

Can I save calculator results as PDF?

Use the Print / Save as PDF button and choose Save as PDF if supported by your browser.

Is the IST clock synchronized with an official time server?

No. It displays your device date and time using the Asia/Kolkata time zone.
