Our Story

Managing everyday financial records can become difficult when information is scattered across notebooks, messages and documents.

MeraHisab is being developed to make financial record management more organized, accessible and understandable.

The project brings together MeraHisab for personal finance and Royal Khata for digital credit and debit records.
