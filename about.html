About MeraHisab

Your Money. Your Control.

Our Purpose

MeraHisab is a personal finance and expense tracking application created to help people organise their daily income, expenses, savings and financial records in one place.

Our goal is to make money management simple, clear and convenient through an easy-to-use experience.

Designed for Everyday Life

Whether you want to record a daily expense, track income, manage different accounts or review your financial activity, MeraHisab aims to make the process organised and easy to understand.

Key Features

💰

Income & Expense Tracking

Record and organise your financial transactions.

🏦

Account Management

Organise records into dedicated accounts and categories.

📊

Reports & Analytics

Understand your financial activity through summaries and graphs.

🔒

Privacy Focus

Designed with local data storage and privacy in mind.

💾

Backup & Restore

Manage your own backup files.

📝

Notes & Planning

Organise notes, tasks and reminders.

Our Vision

We aim to build a reliable, simple and privacy-conscious personal finance tool that helps users stay organised and make better-informed decisions about their money.
