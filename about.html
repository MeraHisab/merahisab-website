Who We Are

MeraHisab is a digital financial management project focused on simplifying everyday financial record keeping.

Our goal is to help individuals, families, shopkeepers and small businesses organize their financial information through practical digital solutions.

Our application portfolio includes MeraHisab for personal finance management and Royal Khata for digital ledger and credit-debit management.

We aim to combine simplicity, accessibility, privacy-conscious design and responsible financial record management.
