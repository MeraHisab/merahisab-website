Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 23, 2026

Introduction

MeraHisab is a personal finance and expense tracking application designed with a privacy-first approach. We value the privacy of our users and aim to keep personal financial information under their control.

Data Collection

The MeraHisab Android application is designed to store financial information locally on the user's device. The app is intended not to require a cloud account or online server for its core expense-tracking functionality.

Financial Information

Income, expenses, account details, categories and related financial records are intended to remain stored on the user's device.

Data Sharing

MeraHisab does not intend to sell users' personal financial information. The application is designed around local data storage and user privacy.

Backup

Users may use the app's backup and restore features to manage their own data files. Users should keep backups in a safe and secure location.

Permissions

Some features may request permissions such as notifications or calendar access when needed. Permissions are intended to support the selected features.

Children's Privacy

MeraHisab is a general personal finance utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the application develops. The latest version will be published on this website.

Contact

Contact details for privacy-related questions will be published here when official support details are available.

Note: This policy describes the intended privacy design. It should be reviewed and updated to match the final released application's actual behavior before publication.
